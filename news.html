Award

NWO Veni grant awarded €320,000

Dr. Abhishek Purandare has been awarded an NWO Veni grant for the project “Unveiling the physics of CO₂ desublimation: advancing cryogenic carbon capture through fundamental phase-change studies.”

August 2026

Website

HiPhy Lab website launched

The HiPhy Lab website is launched to present our research on heat transport and interfacial phase-change physics, including CO₂ sublimation, CO₂ desublimation, phase change at supercritical pressures, and transport in porous media.

2025

Industry project

Industry-funded project on cryogenic carbon capture

We carried out an industry-funded project focused on the energy and mass balance of an existing cryogenic carbon capture unit. The project supported the assessment of system-level performance, material flows, cooling requirements, and opportunities for improving cryogenic CO₂ separation technology.
