Interested in heat, interfaces, and phase change?

We welcome motivated students, researchers, and collaborators interested in heat transfer, fluid mechanics, phase-change physics, cryogenic systems, CO₂ technologies, and thermal energy systems.

Join the lab

Opportunities

PhD candidates and postdoctoral researchers

Funded PhD and postdoctoral positions will be advertised here when available. Candidates with a strong background in heat transfer, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, experimental methods, numerical modelling, or phase-change physics are encouraged to follow the HiPhy Lab updates.

MSc and BSc thesis projects

Students interested in thesis projects can contact Dr. Abhishek Purandare with a short motivation, CV, transcript, and a brief description of their interests. Possible topics include CO₂ sublimation, CO₂ desublimation, supercritical CO₂ heat transfer, cryogenic carbon capture, and transport in porous media.

Internships and visiting students

Visiting students and interns are welcome when there is a good match between the student’s background, project timing, and available supervision. Please include the desired period, funding situation, and the requirements of your home university when contacting us.

What to send

Please send a concise email including your CV, transcript if relevant, preferred starting period, and a few sentences explaining why you are interested in the HiPhy Lab.
